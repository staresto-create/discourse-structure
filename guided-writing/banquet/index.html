填寫範文的空格

每個空格都有「功能」要求，不是只有一個標準答案。先自己寫，再按下方的「送出，看參考寫法」。短的空格沒有提示，請從前後文判斷要填哪一種功能的字，送出後會說明。

開頭可以用：The two pictures show… / The two pictures present… Picture A shows a formal ten-course banquet in a hotel banquet hall. Well-dressed guests sit around a large round table, and waiters serve one dish at a time. Everything follows a fixed order, from the cold appetizers to the final dessert. is common at weddings and company dinners. Picture B, on the other hand, shows an all-you-can-eat buffet, where diners walk freely between food stations and fill their plates with whatever they like. The banquet creates a refined and orderly atmosphere, the buffet feels casual and lively.

開頭可以用：If I had to choose, I would… / I would rather… because… One reason is that everyone can eat exactly what they enjoy, so nobody has to force down a dish they dislike. I can go back for seconds of my favorite dishes, while my friends try small portions of everything. friends can move around freely and chat with different people instead of staying in one seat for two hours. freedom turns a meal into real time together rather than a polite performance. Admittedly, a banquet looks impressive and suits formal occasions, but I value relaxed conversation more than elegant service. 開頭可以用：All things considered, … / In conclusion, … / For these reasons, …

逐格對照

想改答案，可以回到上面修改，再按一次「送出，看參考寫法」，這裡的對照會跟著更新。

以下「參考寫法」只是其中一種。判斷重點是「功能對不對」，不是用字要一模一樣。下面的自動提示只比對開頭幾個字，沒出現 ✓ 不代表你寫錯。

(1) 主題句
The two pictures present two quite different ways of sharing a meal with friends and family.
功能要求如下。
(2) 換字（前面已寫過 banquet，這裡要換一種說法指回它）
This traditional style of dining
可接受：this / such a / that 加上「一種、一類」意思的名詞，例如 This traditional style of dining、This kind of meal、Such a formal meal。後面接 is，所以要用單數。重複寫 banquet 不算文法錯，只是這一格要練習換一種說法。
來源：University of Toronto — Special Cases in the Use of the Definite Article（第二次提到可以換同義說法）、Cambridge — It, this and that in paragraphs
(3) 對比連接詞（同一句內，連接兩個相反的描述）
whereas
可接受：whereas / while / but / yet。前面已有逗號，所以用連接詞而不是 However 這類副詞。
來源：Cambridge — whereas
(4) 主題句
If I had to choose, I would go for the buffet when eating out with friends.
功能要求如下。
(5) 句型接寫（前面是 One reason is that，現在要寫第二個理由）
Another reason is that
最能呼應 One reason is that 的是 Another reason is that 或 A second reason is that（本題庫建議）。Besides, / In addition, / Moreover（較正式）, / What is more, / Furthermore, / Also, 是一般的追加用語，也能帶出第二個理由，只是和 One reason 不成對。Second, 常見，但和 One reason 不成對。注意：用 Another reason is that 時 that 不能漏。
來源：besides、in addition、moreover（Cambridge）
(6) 指涉（前一句講的是 move around freely）
This
可接受：This / That / The / Such（後面是 freedom）。This 指回前一句整件事，最自然。
來源：Cambridge — It, this and that in paragraphs（this 指回前文整句）、University of Toronto（the 指回已提過的名詞）
(7) 結論
All things considered, a buffet is the better choice for a casual meal with friends.
可接受的開頭：All things considered, / All in all, / In conclusion, / In short, / Overall, / To sum up。
