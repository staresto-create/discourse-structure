填寫範文的空格

每個空格都有「功能」要求，不是只有一個標準答案。先自己寫，再按下方的「送出，看參考寫法」。短的空格沒有提示，請從前後文判斷要填哪一種功能的字，送出後會說明。

開頭可以用：The two pictures show… / Picture A shows… while Picture B shows… In Picture A, a group of students are cleaning up a beach. Wearing gloves, they pick up plastic bottles and cans from the sand. Picture B takes place indoors at a community center, where one student is helping a young child with homework. The two sit side by side at a small table. While the former activity calls for physical effort and teamwork, the depends more on patience and one-on-one communication.

If I had to choose, I would volunteer at the community center. For one thing, I enjoy working with children. A child who struggles with schoolwork often feels discouraged. By staying patient and encouraging, I could help child regain confidence. Seeing that child smile again would be the best reward I could ask for. I have tutored my younger cousin for years, so I know how to explain hard ideas clearly. Although cleaning the beach is worthwhile, I believe that sharing what I know with children would make a more lasting difference. 開頭可以用：All in all, … / In conclusion, … / For these reasons, …

逐格對照

以下「參考寫法」只是其中一種。判斷重點是「功能對不對」，不是用字要一模一樣。下面的自動提示只比對開頭幾個字，沒出現 ✓ 不代表你寫錯。

(1) 主題句
The two pictures show two very different ways of doing volunteer work.
(2) 對比轉折（接在 Picture A 的描述後，後面要開始談 Picture B）
In contrast,
可接受的開頭：In contrast, / By contrast, / On the other hand, / However。Meanwhile, 也能用，但容易被讀成「同一時間」，較弱。
來源：Purdue OWL — Transitional Devices、Cambridge — on the other hand
(3) 指涉（前面已有 the former activity）
the latter
latter 和前面的 former 成對，最標準；the other 也通；the second 文法可以，但和 former 不成對，較不理想。
來源：Merriam-Webster — the latter、Dictionary.com — latter、Grammarly — former vs. latter
(4) 指回前面的名詞（前面已經提過 a child）
the
可接受：the（標準）；this、that 也可指前面那個孩子。
來源：University of Toronto — Special Cases in the Use of the Definite Article
(5) 句型接寫（前面是 For one thing，現在要寫第二個理由）
For another,
最能呼應 For one thing 的是 For another, / For another thing。Besides, / In addition, / Moreover, / What is more, / Furthermore, / Also, 是一般的追加用語，也能帶出第二個理由，只是和 For one thing 不成對。Second, 常見但較弱（前面不是 First）。
來源：Cambridge — thing（for one thing … for another 例句）、besides、in addition、moreover
(6) 結論
All in all, tutoring at the community center would be rewarding for me and helpful to the children.
可接受的開頭：All in all, / In conclusion, / In short, / Overall, / To sum up, / For these reasons。
