引導式寫作：介紹台北 101

中等程度約 180 個單詞3 個空格書信

寫作題目

提示：你的外國筆友 Alex 下個月要來台灣旅遊，你想帶他去參觀台北 101。請寫一封英文信給 Alex，文分兩段。第一段說明台北 101 的位置，並描述你自己觀察到的外觀（例如形狀、顏色或細節）；第二段說明你去過那附近的經驗（若沒去過，可寫你在哪裡看過它），以及它對你有什麼特別的意義。信末請以 Ming 署名，不得使用自己的真實姓名。（文長至少 120 個單詞）

這一篇的重點是「觀察」：外觀那一格要寫你自己看得到的樣子，不是把景點介紹的資料抄上去。

填寫範文的空格

每個空格都有「功能」要求，不是只有一個標準答案。先自己寫，再按下方的「送出，看參考寫法」。(1) 位置已經寫好，當作示範，所以空格從 (2) 開始。

Dear Alex,

I'm so glad you're coming to Taiwan next month! There are many places I'd like to show you, but the one I want to take you to most is Taipei 101. It is located in Xinyi District, and is easy to reach by MRT. 開頭可以用：From a distance, it looks like… / It is shaped like… / If you look closely, you will notice that… / At night, …

開頭可以用：Last year, I… / I have been there… times. I usually… / I have never been inside, but I… To me, Taipei 101 is more than a tall building. 開頭可以用：It reminds me of… / It is special to me because… / Every time I see it, I feel… I'm sure you will love it as much as I do. I can't wait to show you around!

Yours,
Ming

逐格對照

想改答案，可以回到上面修改，再按一次「送出，看參考寫法」，這裡的對照會跟著更新。

以下「參考寫法」只是其中一種。判斷重點是「功能對不對」，不是用字要一模一樣。

(2) 外觀（第一段，位置之後）
From a distance, the tower looks like a giant bamboo stalk growing toward the sky. If you look closely, you will notice that it is made up of eight sections that look alike, and each one is a little wider at the top than at the bottom.
可接受的寫法（以下任兩種都可以）：
  • It looks like bamboo. / It is shaped like a tall, thin bamboo plant reaching into the sky.
  • It is made up of eight sections that look the same.
  • Each section is wider at the top than at the bottom.
  • It is much taller than the buildings around it, so you can see it from far away.
  • Its walls are covered with glass, which shines in the sunlight.
  • At night, the building lights up and looks beautiful.
你自己看到的任何具體特徵都算，不必和參考寫法相同（本題庫建議）。
易錯（本題庫建議）：bamboo shoot 是「竹筍」，要寫竹子的樣子請用 bamboo 或 a bamboo stalk；is 和 look 不能連用，要寫 It looks like…；描寫由幾個部分組成用 made up of。
事實來源：交通部觀光署台灣觀光資訊網（造型像竹子、每八層為一個結構單元）、Thornton Tomasetti — Taipei 101（八個重複的單元，每個單元下窄上寬）、李祖原建築師事務所 — Taipei 101
(3) 經驗（第二段前半）
Last New Year's Eve, my friends and I waited in the crowd near the tower for almost three hours. When the clock struck midnight, fireworks shot out from the building, and everyone around us cheered.
可接受的寫法：
  • Last year, I watched the New Year's Eve fireworks there with my family.
  • Last summer, I went up to the observatory and saw the whole city.
  • I have been there three times. I usually go shopping or watch movies nearby.
  • I often meet my friends at the department stores near the tower.
  • I have never been inside, but I often see it from the MRT.
  • I have never been there, but I watch its fireworks on TV every New Year's Eve.
沒去過的話，寫「在哪裡看過它」也算完成。
易錯（本題庫建議）：there 是副詞，I have been to there 要刪掉 to；有 Last year 這種過去的時間，要用過去式：Last year, I went…，不寫 Last year, I have gone…。
來源：台北 101 每年元旦零時施放跨年煙火（NOWnews 今日新聞）
(4) 對你的意義（第二段後半）
It reminds me of that cold but exciting night, and every time I see it, I feel proud of my city.
可接受的寫法：
  • It reminds me of the first time I went there with my parents.
  • It reminds me of the countdown with my best friends.
  • It is special to me because I can see it on my way home every day.
  • It is special to me because it was the first place I visited in Taipei.
  • Every time I see it, I feel proud of my city.
  • Every time I see it, I know I am almost home.
  • It means a lot to me because my family and I go there every year.
只要寫出一個具體的回憶、理由或感受就算完成，不要求寫「象徵」什麼（本題庫建議）。
易錯（本題庫建議）：remind 後面要先接人，再接 of：It reminds me of my childhood，不是 It reminds me my childhood；special、mean a lot 後面接 to me：It is special to me，不寫 It is special for me。
來源：Merriam-Webster — remind (someone) of、Oxford Learner’s — remind of
